01 Current Focus

The Mission

Lookover · Founder · January 2026 – Present

Solving the Trust Gap

The layer enterprises need before they can ship AI agents to production.

The Problem: Enterprises can build AI agents. They can't deploy them. The blocker isn't capability — it's verifiable accountability. CISOs and Legal teams can't sign off on systems they can't audit.

The Product: An asynchronous logger designed for active compliance monitoring and real-time risk flagging — built specifically for production AI agents.

The Thesis: As the EU AI Act moves toward full enforcement in August 2026, audit trails move from "nice to have" to a legal requirement. Lookover is the infrastructure layer for that shift.

Initial Vertical: Customer Support agents — where the risk of hallucination or PII leakage is highest, and where CISOs need a defensible paper trail.
